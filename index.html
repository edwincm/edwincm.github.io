02SECURITY AUTOMATIONGiving incident deadlines a workflowCase-based SLA assignment, milestone tracking, and escalation.n8nIRISMicrosoft TeamsCase study

The problem

Incident response, containment, and resolution have separate deadlines. Tracking them manually can make an approaching deadline easy to miss.

My contribution

Developed workflows for SLA assignment and periodic breach checking with n8n and IRIS. Defined case attributes for due times, milestone status, and escalation, with Teams notifications for warnings and breaches.

What it demonstrates

Translating a security process into explicit state, time-based checks, and actionable notifications.

Scope: workflow implementation; no measured response-time improvement is claimed.

03RESPONSE WORKFLOWSConnecting alerts to the next actionAlert escalation, case updates, and approval-based response steps.ShuffleIRISTeamsPythonCase study

The problem

An alert is only a starting point. Responders also need context, a case record, and a clear way to decide what should happen next.

My contribution

Built and worked with Shuffle workflows connecting alert information, Teams escalation messages, IRIS timeline updates, and user-input steps. Used Python to normalize nested alert data for downstream actions.

What it demonstrates

API integration, payload handling, and approval steps that keep people involved in security response.

Scope: escalation and approval workflow integration; autonomous remediation is not implied.

04VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENTTurning findings into readable reportsFinding review, contextual triage, and remediation recommendations.Vulnerability analysisExcelSecurity reportingCase study

The problem

A list of vulnerabilities does not tell an IT team which findings are relevant, why they matter, or what to review next.

My contribution

Reviewed vulnerability findings for validity, affected packages and versions, relevance, and priority. Prepared detailed assessment reports and sent recommendations to the IT team, reporting persistent findings in subsequent assessment cycles.

What it demonstrates

Technical triage and clear communication between security and IT, supported by structured reporting.

Scope: assessment and recommendations. I did not independently track and verify remediation closure.
